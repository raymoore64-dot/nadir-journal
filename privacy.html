Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

The developer does not collect data through Nadir. Journal entries and analysis are stored on your iPhone.

1. Zero Cloud, Zero Accounts, Zero Tracking

Nadir has no user accounts, no cloud servers, no analytics SDKs, no crash telemetry uploads, and no advertising trackers. Voice transcription and AI features use Apple’s on-device frameworks when supported. Apple may need to download system language assets before offline use.

2. On-Device Storage & Protection

All dream entries, extracted metadata, insights, personal lexicon notes, and preferences are stored exclusively in a local database on your iPhone protected by iOS Complete Data Protection (encrypted when your device is locked), with optional Face ID / Touch ID locking inside the app.

3. On-Device AI & Voice Processing

Speech recognition uses Apple’s on-device Speech framework and discards raw audio buffers immediately after transcription. Dream indexing, pattern synthesis, and archive Q&A run locally using Apple’s on-device Foundation Models and NaturalLanguage frameworks.

4. Optional Read-Only Apple Health Access

If you explicitly enable Sleep Context in Settings, Nadir requests strictly read-only access to Apple Health Sleep Analysis data so it can correlate sleep and REM duration with nightmares and lucid dreams locally on your iPhone. Nadir never writes to Apple Health and never transmits health data off your device.

5. User-Initiated Local Backups

Because Nadir does not sync to a cloud server, you can export a lossless JSON backup or Markdown archive at any time. You choose where to save or share exported files. System backups and destinations such as iCloud Drive are governed by your Apple settings and the destination’s privacy practices.
