Support & FAQ

Everything you need for 3am capture, local backups, and on-device AI.

Which iPhones support Apple Intelligence synthesis?

Nadir requires iOS 26.0 or later. Apple’s built-in on-device Foundation Model runs on iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 16 series, and newer models with Apple Intelligence enabled in iOS Settings. On older iOS 26 iPhones, Nadir automatically runs its local statistical pattern engine, Personal Lexicon, and 3am journal without any cloud dependency—and checks eligibility transparently before you pay.

How can I verify that Nadir never sends my dreams to a server?

Turn on Airplane Mode on your iPhone, log a new dream by voice or text, tap Find Patterns, and ask a question in Ask Your Archive. Every feature works identically completely offline.

How do I bind 3am Capture to the Action Button or Lock Screen?

  • Action Button: Open iOS Settings → Action Button → Shortcut → select Nadir · 3am Dream Capture.
  • Lock Screen Widget: Long-press your Lock Screen → Customize → add the Nadir 3am Quick Capture circular or rectangular widget.
  • Hands-Free Siri: Say “Hey Siri, whisper a dream fragment in Nadir.”

How do I back up or transfer my dreams to a new iPhone?

Because Nadir never syncs to a cloud server, open Settings → Local Export & Backup → Export Full JSON Backup to save a lossless archive to Files. On your new iPhone, tap Restore from JSON Backup.

How do I import existing dreams from Apple Notes, Somnary, or DreamLog?

Go to Settings → Import Past Dreams (or tap the import button on a fresh Journal tab), choose a .md, .txt, or .csv file or paste directly from Notes. Nadir detects date headers and indexes everything locally.
